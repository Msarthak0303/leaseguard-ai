LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is between ABC Properties and John Smith.

The lease term shall be three years.

The monthly rent shall be INR 50,000.

The Tenant shall provide a security deposit equal to six months' rent.

The Tenant shall provide ninety days notice before termination.

The Landlord may increase the rent by eight percent every year.

Maintenance shall be handled appropriately by the parties as required.

The Tenant may sublet the property without obtaining written approval from the Landlord.

The Tenant shall pay a late fee of seven percent for delayed rent.

The Landlord may terminate the agreement at any time at the Landlord's sole discretion.

The Tenant agrees to waive all claims against the Landlord.

Signed by the parties.
