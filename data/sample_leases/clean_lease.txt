LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is between ABC Properties as Landlord and John Smith as Tenant.

The lease term shall be two years.

The monthly rent shall be INR 50,000.

The Tenant shall provide a security deposit equal to two months' rent.

Either party may terminate the lease by providing sixty days written notice.

The Landlord may increase the rent by five percent annually.

The Landlord shall be responsible for structural maintenance and major repairs.

The security deposit shall be returned to the Tenant within thirty days after the lease ends, subject to lawful deductions.

The Tenant may sublet the property only with prior written approval from the Landlord.

The Tenant shall pay a late fee not exceeding five percent of the overdue rent.

Either party may terminate the agreement according to the termination conditions specified in this agreement.

Signed by the Landlord and Tenant.
